What Swingbus does

Two tools for one study

A grid-connection study asks two things: does the plant's simulation model behave like the real plant, and what grid did each test see? The Grid Plotter answers the first and the Grid Calculator the second. Both run on your PC with the files you already have.

Grid Plotter

Compare the simulation with the field

  • Overlay PSCAD, PSS®E, COMTRADE, MATLAB and CSV runs on the same axes
  • Zoom to the fault and read every signal at two cursors, with the difference
  • Turn the error between two signals into a signal of its own
  • Min, max and mean per signal, and one tab for each test case

Grid Calculator

Work out the grid behind the test

  • SCR and X/R to the source impedance Zs, in ohms and per unit
  • The fault impedance Zf for a voltage dip or swell, with AEMO DMAT values
  • 1PG, 2PG and LL faults by symmetrical components
  • The SCR the machine sees at its terminal, through the transformer

Simulation and measurement on one screen

Open PSCAD, PSS®E, COMTRADE, MATLAB or CSV files. Overlay them, zoom to the fault, read two cursors, and keep one tab per test case.

The grid behind your test, worked out

  • Grid impedance: SCR and X/R to Zs in ohms and per unit, on four bases.
  • Fault impedance: the Zf for a voltage dip or swell, with AEMO DMAT values, and the voltage plotted along the circuit, against Zf and over time.
  • Unbalanced faults: 1PG, 2PG and LL by symmetrical components.
  • Bases and the transformer: the SCR the machine sees at its terminal.
